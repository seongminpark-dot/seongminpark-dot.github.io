Project Overview

This website analyzes how MKBHD’s review of the Sony WH 1000XM6 noise cancelling headphones creates meaning through visual rhetoric, mise en scène, montage, expert narration, and audience metrics.

The project connects consumer technology with visual communication by examining how a YouTube review presents noise cancelling headphones as advanced, trustworthy, and desirable.
